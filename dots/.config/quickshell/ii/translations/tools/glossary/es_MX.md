# Mexican Spanish terminology

Current display-name policy and all 19 locale decisions are in
[TERMINOLOGY.md](../../TERMINOLOGY.md). That consolidated guide supersedes earlier
English-preservation instructions in historical notes.


Use concise Mexican software Spanish, tú in instructions, infinitives for actions,
and sentence case. Existing sound translations determine terminology. Inflect terms
for grammar; retain literal commands, product names, shortcuts and placeholders.
These are translation decisions, without native-speaker review.
