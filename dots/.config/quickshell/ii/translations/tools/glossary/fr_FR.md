# French UI terminology

Current display-name policy and all 19 locale decisions are in
[TERMINOLOGY.md](../../TERMINOLOGY.md). That consolidated guide supersedes earlier
English-preservation instructions in historical notes.


Use French for France and the existing polite `vous` register. Short labels use
nouns or infinitives with sentence case. Keep existing sound translations. This
glossary records translation decisions, not native-speaker review. Preserve names,
command arguments, units, format tokens and user content. Use count-neutral labels
where a placeholder may be 1. French punctuation may take a nonbreaking space
inside the string; preserve the source's joining whitespace at its boundaries.
