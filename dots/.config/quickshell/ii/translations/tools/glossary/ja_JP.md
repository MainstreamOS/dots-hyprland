# Japanese UI terminology

Current display-name policy and all 19 locale decisions are in
[TERMINOLOGY.md](../../TERMINOLOGY.md). That consolidated guide supersedes earlier
English-preservation instructions in historical notes.


Use standard Japanese with です/ます in explanations and concise noun or action
labels. Preserve sound existing translations. Keep executable names, identifiers,
URLs, paths, units and user content intact. Use Japanese punctuation in prose,
but ASCII quotes for literal text the user types. Preserve actual line breaks and
joining whitespace. Japanese counts do not require plural inflection. This
records translation choices, not native-speaker verification.
