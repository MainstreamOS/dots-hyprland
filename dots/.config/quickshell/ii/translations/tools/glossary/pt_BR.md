# Brazilian Portuguese terminology

Current display-name policy and all 19 locale decisions are in
[TERMINOLOGY.md](../../TERMINOLOGY.md). That consolidated guide supersedes earlier
English-preservation instructions in historical notes.


Use Brazilian software Portuguese and você in instructions, with infinitives for
actions and sentence case. Preserve existing good translations, executable syntax,
names and placeholders. Translate remaining English workspace terminology as Área
de trabalho; Desktop is the desktop environment or desktop surface in context.
These decisions have not received native-speaker review.
