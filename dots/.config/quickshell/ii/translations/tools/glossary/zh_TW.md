# Traditional Chinese UI terminology (Taiwan)

Current display-name policy and all 19 locale decisions are in
[TERMINOLOGY.md](../../TERMINOLOGY.md). That consolidated guide supersedes earlier
English-preservation instructions in historical notes.


Standard Traditional Chinese for Taiwan (繁體中文 / 臺灣). Retains established
computing conventions in Taiwan (視窗, 檔案, 資料夾, 應用程式, 偏好設定, 記憶體,
螢幕, 網路, 支援, 伺服器, 系統匣, 捷徑, 重新開機, 登出, 登入, etc.).
Preserves command syntax, identifiers, URLs, paths, units, and joining whitespace.
Traditional Chinese count expressions naturally fit without plural inflection.
Never convert Simplified Chinese mechanically; adhere to Taiwan terminology.
